## Overview

This interactive **Customer-Facing Value Selling & Technical PoC Simulator** quantifies the tangible operational, financial, and technical gains that an enterprise customer achieves by connecting their workflows on **Autodesk's Design & Make Platform** and automating data pipelines via **Autodesk Platform Services (APS) REST APIs**.

Rather than focusing on software vendor licensing metrics, this tool is built to help the **customer's own buying committee (CFO, CTO, and VP of Engineering/Production)** build an internal business case around **cost avoidance, reclaimed engineering hours, and faster time-to-market**.

---

## What the Customer Gains (Key Modules)

1. **Industry-Specific Customer Impact:**
   - **Media & Entertainment (Flow Cloud):** Eliminates rendering/review bottlenecks and cuts studio approval loops by 35%.
   - **AEC & Infrastructure (Autodesk Construction Cloud / Forma):** Detects BIM clashes in the cloud before construction starts, avoiding costly on-site rework.
   - **Manufacturing (Fusion / PLM):** Connects engineering BOMs directly to factory CAM and supplier ERP via APS APIs, slashing physical prototyping scrap by 38%.

2. **Quantifiable Customer KPIs:**
   - **Annual Cost Savings (€):** Direct savings from rework elimination, scrap avoidance, and legacy IT consolidation.
   - **Engineering / Creative Hours Reclaimed:** Thousands of hours saved annually by eliminating manual file imports/exports and version tracking.
   - **Project Delivery Acceleration:** Percentage reduction in end-to-end project cycle times.

3. **C-Suite Buying Center Views:**
   - **Customer CFO:** Rework cost avoidance, elimination of 22% idle license waste ("shelfware") via global follow-the-sun pooling, and payback period.
   - **Customer CTO / CIO:** Automated CAD/BIM-to-ERP data pipelines via **APS REST APIs & Webhooks** within predictable monthly **API Quotas**.
   - **VP of Engineering / Production:** Single source of truth and hours saved per specialist per week.
